# Shared-walker observations
The home page has a visible Book appointment link. Opening it shows service choices. Choosing a service shows available dates; the chosen service disappears from view. A visitor can return to the service list using browser Back, but then the selected date is cleared. After a slot is selected, the review page shows service, date and price together. Confirm booking was not activated because it writes. A disabled Group bookings control says Coming soon. No screen-reader or keyboard-only walk was performed. No task timing or analytics were captured.
